About

INNOWAVE
2025

Innowave 2025 is the official Entrepreneurship Summit of CHRIST (Deemed to be University), a two-day long event encompassing startups, incubation, entrepreneurship and everything in between. The summit, spanning the entirety of CHRIST (Deemed to be University)'s 28-acre campus, will host numerous activities and programmes. It will bring to the fore entrepreneurs, innovators, incubators, speakers and investors to celebrate and bolster the entrepreneurial ecosystem.

The summit, which will delve into the profound depths of entrepreneurship culture, will conduct activities like engrossing panel discussions, informative masterclasses, engaging speaker sessions, job and placement fares, cut-throat competitions, investors' meet and much more! The summit, open to students and people from various disciplines and fields, will allow them to come together and explore their innovative ideas and put their entrepreneurial skills to test. It will enable them to learn from the very best, attend educational sessions, partake in intense competitions and network with other whiz entrepreneurs.

THE OBJECTIVES




INNOFAIR aims to bridge the gap between student entrepreneurs and key stakeholders in the business ecosystem.
INNOFAIR 2025 aspires to celebrate the entrepreneurial journey of students while fostering an ecosystem of innovation and growth.
It invites professionals, investors, and enthusiasts to witness the pioneering efforts of the next generation of leaders.

ABOUT THE HOST




CHRIST Incubation Centre, also known as the CHRIST Incubation and Entrepreneurship Support Cell, is the official incubation centre of CHRIST (Deemed to be University). The centre encourages, nurtures and supports young and aspiring entrepreneurs to activate their innovative minds, chalk up creative ideas and turn them into successful businesses. The incubation centre firmly believes that entrepreneurship leads to economic growth and progress, and works to provide budding entrepreneurs with all the resources they need. The centre imparts access to resources, mentorship, guidance, learning, networking and other amazing opportunities.

PROPEL

Propel is a beacon of innovation, transcending the traditional boundaries of competition. More than just an event, it represents a transformative platform meticulously crafted to nurture the entrepreneurial spirit of India's most brilliant young minds. Hosted by Innowave'25, this premier business plan pitching competition is a crucible where raw creativity meets strategic thinking, where visionary ideas are forged into potential market-disrupting solutions.

THE VISION


Propel cultivates a culture of innovation, bridging the gap between academic knowledge and real-world challenges. By empowering aspiring entrepreneurs, Propel provides a launchpad for transformative ideas, connecting innovators with industry leaders to shape a brighter future. This platform fosters a vibrant community of problem-solvers, driving positive change.

COMPETITION STRUCTURE

Propel unfolds across an intense two-day entrepreneurial journey, meticulously designed to test and showcase participants' strategic thinking, resilience, and innovation.
